A practical intelligence network for people making decisions across markets, policy shifts, and geopolitical change.

How DuckDuck Club Works

The club runs in two layers. Base gives you clear strategic orientation. Premium adds the operating layer: deeper access, sharper context, and support for execution.

Base

Your strategic baseline.

  • Core: A concise weekly view of what matters now, without noise.
  • Direction: Priority themes so you can decide what deserves attention next.
  • Positioning: Frameworks for where to lean in, hold, or stay out.
  • Curated signal: Selected developments with clear implications, not headline volume.

Premium

Execution context and operational depth.

  • The Sanctum: Closed strategy briefings that connect events to practical moves.
  • Duck Tank: Candid scenario sessions where assumptions are pressure-tested.
  • Black Book: A vetted network map of relevant operators, specialists, and decision channels.
  • Global Moves: Real-time interpretation of cross-border shifts and second-order effects.
  • Language Arsenal: Ready-to-use messaging and negotiation language for high-stakes contexts.
