How DuckDuck Club Works

DuckDuck Club runs in two layers. Base gives you clear direction and signal you can act on. Premium adds the operational context behind those signals so you can execute with more confidence and precision.

Layer 1

Base

The core layer for members who want strong positioning and dependable signal without noise.

  • Direction: What to pay attention to now, and why it matters.
  • Positioning: How to frame your next move relative to market conditions.
  • Curated Signal: Filtered opportunities and risks, ranked by practical relevance.

Layer 2

Premium

The operational layer for members who need deeper access, decision support, and context for higher-stakes execution.

  • The Sanctum: Private strategic briefings that explain the reasoning behind current plays.
  • Duck Tank: Live scenario reviews where ideas are pressure-tested before capital is deployed.
  • Black Book: An actionable repository of vetted frameworks, contacts, and playbooks.
  • Global Moves: Cross-market intelligence that maps regional events to portfolio implications.
  • Language Arsenal: Clear communication templates for negotiations, updates, and investor-facing narratives.
